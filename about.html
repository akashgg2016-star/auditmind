01 · Why it exists

The real challenge is turning a large regulatory universe into a usable audit view.

The reference material spans entity classification, scale-based supervision, prudential requirements, governance, compliance, disclosures and reporting. AuditMind uses these information patterns as inspiration, but creates its own wording, hierarchy and visual language.

03 · Product thinking

Not another PDF. A working interface.

Decision trees, threshold tables, applicability matrices and process diagrams in regulatory material are useful patterns. AuditMind will convert those patterns into interactive, original audit experiences.

APPLICABILITY

Know what applies first.

A user should be able to establish the regulatory context before opening detailed requirements.

  • Classification and scale
  • Business and customer context
  • Applicable control families
  • Priority areas for audit planning
